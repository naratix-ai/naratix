# Reports: how the shop is doing

Read before answering, and report the numbers in plain words with the link to the page they come from:

- `dashboard-summary`: the shop at a glance, and its setup checklist while it is getting started.
- `analytics-summary`: one taxonomy's quality.
- `list-runs` with `kind: activity`: what the shop ran, narrowed with `search`, `failures_only` or `since` for "what failed this week".

**Completion:** every number you report comes from one of these reads, with its page's link.
