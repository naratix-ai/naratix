# Naratix plugin

Get every product in your catalogue ready to sell, from Claude or ChatGPT. The plugin connects your AI assistant to your [Naratix](https://naratix.ai) shop: it brings in products and taxonomies, places products in categories, enriches their attributes, improves their photos, writes titles, descriptions and SEO text, checks quality, and sends the results to your sales channels. It lives in [`naratix/`](naratix/); its [README](naratix/README.md) says what it does, what it sends where, and how to install it in each app.

Step by step for each app: [api.naratix.ai/connect](https://api.naratix.ai/connect).

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add naratix-ai/naratix
/plugin install naratix@naratix
```
