# Naratix plugin

Get every product in your catalogue ready to sell, from Claude or ChatGPT. The plugin connects your AI assistant to your [Naratix](https://naratix.ai) shop: it brings in products and taxonomies, places products in categories, enriches their attributes, improves their photos, writes titles, descriptions and SEO text, checks quality, and sends the results to your sales channels. It lives in [`naratix/`](naratix/); its [README](naratix/README.md) says how to get an account, how to connect each app, what to ask, and what happens to your data and keys.

New to Naratix? Create an account at [api.naratix.ai/app/register](https://api.naratix.ai/app/register); it comes with an empty shop to start in.

Step by step for each app: [api.naratix.ai/connect](https://api.naratix.ai/connect). The same guide is in [the Naratix app](https://api.naratix.ai/app): press the **AI apps** button (the robot icon at the top right).

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin marketplace add naratix-ai/naratix
/plugin install naratix@naratix
```
